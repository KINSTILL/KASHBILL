# Privacy Policy

**Version 1.0 — Effective November 9, 2026**

## 1. Overview

MyCashBill SARL ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

## 2. Data We Collect

- **Account Data**: Phone number, hashed PIN code, business name, address, tax ID.
- **Billing Data**: Client names, descriptions of services, amounts, invoice dates.
- **Technical Data**: Device type, IP address, operating system, localized preferences.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

- Providing and maintaining the invoicing service.
- Processing subscription payments.
- Preventing fraud and detecting multi-device abuse.
- Customer support and service notifications.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell your data. We only share data with trusted service providers:
- Payment processors (Notch Pay) for transaction processing.
- Database and cloud hosting providers for secure data storage.

## 5. Data Retention and Security

Data is retained for as long as your account is active. Invoices are stored securely and encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest.

## 6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, export, or delete your personal data at any time via your account settings or by contacting privacy@mycashbill.app.
