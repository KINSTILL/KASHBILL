# Refund Policy

**Last updated:** November 9, 2026 · **Version 1.0**

---

## PREAMBLE

This Policy defines the conditions under which a refund may be granted to a User of MyCashBill (published by Dany Mouelnjock, sole proprietor, Douala, NIU P118816334425R). It supplements the Terms of Sale accessible at https://mycashbill.app/en/legal/cgv.

**This Policy applies exclusively to paid subscriptions (Pro plan and add-ons).**

---

## ARTICLE 1 — GENERAL PRINCIPLE: NO REFUND

**As a general rule, NO REFUND is granted after the start of the subscribed period**, except in the cases exhaustively listed in Article 2.

**The Customer acknowledges having been informed of this rule prior to subscription.**

---

## ARTICLE 2 — LIMITED REFUND CASES

### 2.1 — Duplicate payment

In case of multiple debits for the same period, the overpayment is refunded in full. Procedure: request to support@mycashbill.app with both Notch Pay references. Verification within 7 days. Refund within 30 days.

### 2.2 — Technical billing error attributable to the Publisher

In case of an obvious billing error (incorrect amount, wrong currency, erroneous tariff) **exclusively attributable to the Publisher**, the difference is refunded. Verification within 7 days. Refund within 30 days.

### 2.3 — Prolonged Service unavailability

In case of **continuous** unavailability exceeding **15 consecutive days** exclusively attributable to the Publisher, a **pro rata** refund for unused days may be granted.

**Clarification:** unavailability resulting from force majeure, technical provider failure or Customer misuse **does NOT entitle to a refund**.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — CASES EXPRESSLY EXCLUDED FROM REFUND

The following **NEVER** entitle to a refund:

- Change of mind after subscription.
- Partial use or non-use of the Service.
- Dissatisfaction with features, user experience or performance.
- Account suspension for breach of Terms of Service or Terms of Sale.
- Account closure by the Customer during the active period.
- Loss of documents due to failure of retention by the Customer.
- Mobile Money transaction fees charged by the operator.
- Unavailability resulting from force majeure or a technical provider.
- Unavailability resulting from Customer misuse.
- Expiration of the subscription not renewed in time.
- Unwanted renewal (reminder: renewal is manual, no automatic extension).
- Data loss due to a bug, technical incident, or deletion by the User.

---

## ARTICLE 4 — CLAIM PROCEDURE

Any request must be addressed to **support@mycashbill.app** within a maximum of **7 calendar days** following the disputed payment, including:

- Invoice number (`MCB-SUB-XXXX-XXXXXX`)
- Proof of payment (Notch Pay receipt, screenshot)
- Specific reason (consistent with Article 2)
- Payment method used

**Any late or incomplete request is inadmissible.**

---

## ARTICLE 5 — PROCESSING AND TERMS

### 5.1 — Processing time

Acknowledgment of receipt within 48 business hours. Decision within **7 days** of receipt of the complete request.

### 5.2 — Methods

By the same payment method, in CFA francs, within **30 days** after acceptance.

### 5.3 — Fees

The Publisher **charges no fees** on refunds. Any bank or Mobile Money fees remain at the Customer's expense.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — NO RIGHT OF WITHDRAWAL

**The professional Customer expressly acknowledges not benefiting from a right of withdrawal.** As the Service is performed immediately upon payment, no withdrawal period applies. Compliant with Article 7 of the Terms of Sale.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — DISPUTES

In case of persistent disagreement, written complaint to support@mycashbill.app. Response within 15 days.

Failing amicable solution, the dispute shall be brought **exclusively** before the **competent courts of Douala, Cameroon**, in accordance with Article 16 of the Terms of Sale and Article 20 of the Terms of Service.

The Customer acknowledges having:

- read and understood all of this Policy;
- been informed of the general principle of no refund;
- been informed of the 3 limited refund cases;
- been informed of the 7-day period to submit a request;
