# Refund Policy

**Version 1.0 — Effective November 9, 2026**

## 1. Principle

Our goal is your complete satisfaction. We offer a 30-day free trial on the Pro plan with no credit card required so you can test all features risk-free before paying.

## 2. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds for paid subscriptions may be granted in the following cases:
- Double charging or technical payment failure.
- Major service unavailability exceeding 48 consecutive hours attributable solely to our infrastructure.
- Refund requests submitted within 7 days of the first paid charge, provided fewer than 5 documents were generated during the paid period.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items

- Renewals after the initial 7-day period.
- Accounts terminated for violation of our Terms of Service (fraud, illegal activities).
- Consumable paid add-ons that have already been used.

## 4. Refund Process

To request a refund, contact us at billing@mycashbill.app with your transaction reference. Approved refunds are returned via the original payment method (Mobile Money or credit card) within 5 business days.
