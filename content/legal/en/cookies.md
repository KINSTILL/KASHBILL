# Cookie Policy

**Version 1.0 — Effective November 9, 2026**

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and local storage are small text files stored on your device that help web applications function smoothly and remember your preferences.

## 2. Cookies We Use

- **Strictly Necessary**: Session authentication tokens, security and CSRF tokens, localized language preferences.
- **Functional**: Temporary invoice demo prefill (15 min TTL), returning visitor flags.
- **No Third-Party Advertising Cookies**: We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

## 3. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies will prevent authentication and document saving.
