## ARTICLE 1 — WHAT IS A COOKIE?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device when visiting a website. It allows the site to remember information about your visit.

Cookies contain **no sensitive personal information** (no PIN, no banking data).

## ARTICLE 4 — COOKIES WE DO NOT USE

**No data is resold to advertisers.**

## ARTICLE 5 — MANAGING COOKIES

### 5.1 — Cookie banner

On your first visit, a banner allows you to: **Accept all**, **Essential only**, or **Customize**. Your choice is stored in `mycashbill_consent` for 1 year.

### 5.2 — Changing your choice

At any time by clicking "Manage cookies" in the footer, by deleting the `mycashbill_consent` cookie, or via browser settings.

### 5.3 — Browser settings

You can configure your browser to refuse all cookies, accept only certain cookies, be warned on each cookie deposit, or delete all cookies on close.

**Warning:** refusing essential cookies makes the Service unusable.
